Voices in Verse — 诗中有声：意象、意境与诗歌表达
CreativityGrade 7–8Unit 48 Weeks · 32 Lessons
Key Concept

Creativity

Related Concepts

Form · Style · Perspective

Global Context

Personal and Cultural Expression

Statement of Inquiry

Creativity in poetic form, imagery and language creates meaning and expresses emotions and perspectives.

Inquiry Questions

Factual

  • What are the key features of poetry?
  • What are imagery, atmosphere and symbolism?

Conceptual

  • How do poets use imagery and language to create meaning?
  • How can the same image have different meanings?

Debatable

  • Can a poem have more than one valid interpretation?
Learner Profile
  • Thinker: analyse poetic choices and develop evidence-based interpretations.
  • Open-minded: consider multiple interpretations.
  • Communicator: express and support interpretations clearly.
  • Reflective: reflect on how understanding develops.
Objectives
  • Criterion A: Analyse imagery, language, form and literary techniques and explain how they create meaning.
  • Criterion B: Organise ideas logically and support analysis with relevant textual evidence.
  • Criterion D: Use appropriate literary terminology and accurate language to communicate interpretations.

Critical thinking

  • Gather and organise relevant information to formulate an argument.
  • Analyse complex concepts and synthesise them to create new understanding.

Creative thinking

  • Apply existing knowledge to generate new ideas, products or processes.
Summative Assessment

Timed Unseen Poetry Analysis

Students independently analyse an unfamiliar poem within a set time, using relevant textual evidence to explain how poetic choices create meaning and communicate emotions and perspectives.

Criteria: A, B, D
